CreatixReach / Motion studies

Pages that move like film.

Each build is one static HTML page driven entirely by scroll position. No video file, no 3D engine, no build step. Open one and scroll - forwards or backwards, the camera follows you either way.

01 Meridian Freight An overnight haul told in four shots. The camera tracks the truck, closes on the cab window, and goes through it. Logistics / Courier Live 02 Halcyon Six acts inside the walls of an infested house. Scroll it backwards and the colony un-digs itself. Pest control / US & UK Live

Meridian Freight and Halcyon are fictional brands, built to demonstrate scroll-driven art direction. Nothing here is a real client.
