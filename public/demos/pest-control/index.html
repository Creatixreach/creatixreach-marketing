Field guide · what Halcyon treats

Six residents you did not invite.

01

German cockroach

Blattella germanica

Breeds faster than any other indoor roach. Left alone, one female and her descendants put tens of thousands into a building inside a year.

02

Bed bug

Cimex lectularius

Flat enough to live in a seam. Goes months between meals, so an empty room is not an empty room.

03

Pharaoh ant

Monomorium pharaonis

The reason DIY sprays make things worse. Disturb a colony and it buds: one nest becomes four, in four different rooms.

04

Silverfish

Lepisma saccharinum

Eats starch: wallpaper paste, book bindings, the glue in your skirting. Damp is the invitation, not dirt.

05

Subterranean termite

Reticulitermes flavipes

Never breaks the surface. Builds shelter tubes up the foundation and eats the timber from the inside out.

06

Noble false widow

Steatoda nobilis

The UK's most frequently reported biting spider, and the one most often mistaken for something far worse.

Treatment

We come in at their level.

Targeted application into the void, not a fog across your kitchen. The colony is treated where it actually lives — cavity, sub-floor, service run — so it does not simply move two metres left.

Aftercare

Then we give it back.

A treated building is not a sterile one. Halcyon uses the smallest effective dose, in the smallest possible place, so everything that should be in your garden still is.
